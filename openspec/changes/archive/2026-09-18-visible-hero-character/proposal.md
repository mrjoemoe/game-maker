## Why

The run hero is a tiny letter badge, easy to lose among icons and coins. Playtesting needs a distinct character token that reads as a person standing on the tile.

## What Changes

- Render the run-mode hero as a facing character figure in the tile’s piece slot
- Keep other pieces as compact pawns
- Name the occupying piece in the tile’s accessible label

## Impact

- Capability: `playtest-web-app`
- Engine: reused
- Components: `rules/programmed-run` reused (same hero piece; presentation only)
