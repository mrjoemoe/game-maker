## Why

Sword and shield caches clutter the board and are path-over dead ends; gear is already available from the Mage, shops, and villains.

## What Changes

- Remove sword-cache and shield-cache tile types and placements from Goblin Woods
- Place two additional forests in their place (forest count 6 → 8)
- Update rulebook / README / specs that mention caches
