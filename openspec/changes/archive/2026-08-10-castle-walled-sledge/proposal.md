## Why

The castle is too easy to approach once found. It should be sealed by side walls on every edge so a sledgehammer is required to break in.

## What Changes

- Random tile placements MAY set explicit side walls on the placed cell.
- Goblin Woods castle is placed with walls on all four sides; its pass item is the sledgehammer.
- Sledgehammer consume-on-use happens on successful traverse (like other pass items) so breaking in and winning still works in one step.
