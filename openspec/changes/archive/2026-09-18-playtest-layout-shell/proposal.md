## Why

Run-mode chrome is a tall stack (header, toolbar, HUD, actions, board, inventory, tally, debug) that feels cramped and easy to lose. A denser shell keeps the board as the focus without overlapping panels.

## What Changes

- Compact the playtest header and widen the run-mode page
- Place HUD beside the toolbar
- Put inventory and tally/debug in a two-column under-board row on wide screens
- Keep the ten action slots on one row with horizontal scroll instead of wrapping onto two rows

## Impact

- Capability: `playtest-web-app`
- Engine: reused
- Components: none
