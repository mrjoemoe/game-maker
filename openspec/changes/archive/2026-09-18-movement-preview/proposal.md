## Why

Queued walks are invisible on the board until Run, so it is hard to see where a click will send the hero. The character also pops between cells with no motion cue.

## What Changes

- Highlight queued walk cells and a hover preview of the next click’s walk
- Pulse the hero token while a program step is executing
- Face the hero toward the move being applied

## Impact

- Capability: `playtest-web-app`
- Engine: reused
- Components: none
