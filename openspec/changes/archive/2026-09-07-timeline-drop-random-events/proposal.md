## Why

Draw random and the random-event pile add a second deck the playtest does not need. The action cards are enough.

## What Changes

- Remove the random-event deck, the “Random Event” draw card, and every random-event card.
- Remove the Draw random control. Debug draw is action cards only.
- Update the rulebook so a turn no longer forces a random event.

## Impact

- **modified:** `core/timeline` (card catalog)
- **modified:** timeline engine (single action deck)
- **modified:** `playtest-web-app` (hand draws)
- **modified:** `timeline-game-variant` (rulebook)
- **reused:** remaining action card families
