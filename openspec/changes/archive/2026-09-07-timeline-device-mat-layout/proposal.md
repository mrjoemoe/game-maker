## Why

The player mat is too narrow, devices sit beside the hand, and blueprints do not show remaining uses. Device slots should read as cards with use tokens, and each blueprint needs the right deck count, uses, and crystal cost to run.

## What Changes

- Player mat takes **2/3** of the lower playtest row; hand (with devices under it) takes **1/3**.
- Three device slots are card-sized and show remaining uses as tokens.
- Blueprint cards show their use tokens.
- Device uses, blueprint copies, and crystal-to-run costs match the table (Jumper: 3 uses / 12 blueprints / 1 crystal).

## Impact

- **modified:** `core/timeline` (device uses, crystals, blueprint copies)
- **modified:** playtest layout (mat, hand, slots, tokens)
- **modified:** Timeline Game rulebook
- **reused:** eight devices
