## Why

Armed devices only change a quiet toolbar sentence, so it is easy to miss which tool is live and what to click next.

## What Changes

- When a device (or a hand card) is armed, show a sticky top banner with the device letter/name, step, and how-to.
- Highlight the armed device in the dock; Cancel / Escape still clears it.
