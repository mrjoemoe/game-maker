## Why

Run-mode actions are split between a right-hand bank and a track below the board, so composing a program means scanning two distant regions. A single top actions panel keeps charting, undo, and run in one place.

## What Changes

- Combine the action bank and action track into one panel above the board
- Remove the run-mode right sidebar
- Keep inventory, tally, and debug under the board

## Impact

- Capability: `playtest-web-app`
- Engine: reused
- Components: none
