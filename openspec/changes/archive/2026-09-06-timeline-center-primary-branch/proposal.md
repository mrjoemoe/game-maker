## Why

The first timeline sits against the left edge, so new branches can only grow right. It should start in the middle with room to fork both ways.

## What Changes

- Place the primary branch in the horizontal center of the timestream.
- Lay later branches to the left and right of their parent instead of always appending on the right.

## Impact

- **modified:** `playtest-web-app` (timestream layout)
- **reused:** branch parent links already on timeline state
